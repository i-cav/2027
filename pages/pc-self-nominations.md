Dear colleagues,

The 39th International Conference on Computer Aided Verification (CAV 2027) welcomes self-nominations for Program Committee (PC) members.
Researchers working in verification and related areas are invited to submit their nomination by **October 10, 2026**.
Selection will consider relevant expertise and the overall balance of the committee.

Nominations can be made by filling this form: [https://forms.gle/VKJ3CBLDWctrDMqb9](https://forms.gle/VKJ3CBLDWctrDMqb9)

## Contact

For any questions please contact the PC chairs via [cav2027@lists.utwente.nl](mailto:cav2027@lists.utwente.nl).
